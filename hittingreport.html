    Overall Report Summary:

    “Tanner Scott wants chases on sliders down and away. Be disciplined early, hunt the fastball in the zone, and only expand with two strikes.”

    This chart shows Scott's pitch arsenal, average MPH, and percentage thrown to righty hitters. He only throws 2 pitches - and has above average in velo for both.

    2.) Strike Zone Heatmap

    This is a heatmap of Tanner Scott’s pitches to right handed hitters. His fastball lives mostly middle-up and away, and he locates his slider down and away.

    3.) Movement Profile Visualization

    This graphic shows Scott's arm angle and the break direction of his pitches. Fastballs are red, sliders are yellow. He has roughly a ¾ arm slot, his fastball stays up, and his slider breaks in.

    4.) Walk Rate Note

    In 2024, Tanner Scott was in the 7th percentile for walk rate - can be wild at times. Be selective until you get two strikes (protect mode).

    5.) Chase Rate Note

    Scott thrives on batters chasing at pitches not in the strike zone - specifically a 41.4% slider chase rate. Do not chase a low and away slider - wait for either a fastball in the strike zone or a hanging slider in the zone.
